# Fergie Time (the design system)

Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 19 Nov 2026, unchanged, so only the sessions that need it load it.


`../Fergie Time Design System/` — the exported Claude Design system (tokens, 15 component
mirrors, the three lens prototype templates) — is **local-only and unreleased**; Beni is
holding it back until he's satisfied with its quality. References to it in `docs/` are
deliberate teasers, not broken links — leave them be, and never commit the folder into this
repo. `src/index.css` + `src/lib/competitions.ts` remain the token source of truth; the DS
mirrors them, not the reverse.

**The second edition's design record is public (Beni, Sat 14 Nov 2026).** `docs/design/` holds
the overhaul's design week as it was drawn: pen.dev board exports as WebP previews, the ten
picked photographs as previews, the 14 GLSL shaders, Claude Design's second-edition handoff and
its rules note, and the fal record with its cost fields and download links removed. Its images
carry their own notice (`docs/design/LICENCE.md`), not MIT. Still local, and never committed:
the `.pen` files (only pen.dev opens them, and they link to about 280 MB of images by path), the
full-size and rejected photographs, the Claude Design canvas, the design-system folder above,
and board B7, the pay-out concept D6 fenced. A later board or photograph joins the record as a
preview under the same notice, never as a source file.
