**The milestone.** Kickoff learns the European week: a Champions League matchday is a first-class citizen of the Fixtures tab in all three lenses, the 36-club league-phase table lives in the Table tab under the same language as the five domestic tables, and Moments, a hand-curated, linked, empty-first content class, becomes the third tab. **Why it is worth doing now.** The league phase began 8–10 Sep 2026 and the app holds zero Champions League rows while the provider it already uses serves all of them. Matchday 2 is 13–14 Oct 2026; the release should be deployed before it, and no date is promised. **What "stunning" means here, so nobody reaches for a redesign.** Fergie Time's voice, extended: identity from the ⭐ glyph, the 3 px marquee rail and the day-level amber provenance line, never from a hue change; a table whose three bands read at a glance and whose region header stays put under the column header while a sixteen-row band scrolls; a Moments tab whose empty state is the primary artboard and whose first populated card looks like it belongs to the same app; and honesty that is visible, a matchday labelled COMPUTED and a stadium clock that says "local time not known" rather than lying by an hour. Domestic matchday markers: scope, not principle, per the canvas. Strength of schedule and an opponents lane: a different product. Any hue change and in-app playback: ruled out by the design. ### W1 — Venue time zones, for every competition (ruling 1; finding 18; ideas row 42). Staff lane: data.

**The defect, reproduced.** `scripts/providers/espn.ts` line 166 sets `venueTz: COMPETITIONS[competition].tz` for every event, so with `ucl: { tz: 'Europe/Zurich' }` (`src/lib/competitions.ts` line 14) six of the eighteen matchday-1 events render a stadium clock one hour wrong through the real `fixtureTimes` (AEK Athens 18:45 shown as 19:45; Porto, Liverpool, Sporting and Manchester United 21:00 shown as 20:00 in the other direction; Fenerbahçe 18:45 as 19:45). **Build.** A hand-authored `VENUE_TZ_BY_COUNTRY` reference table beside `LEAGUE_TABLES` in `src/lib/competitions.ts` (reference data in the `tv` and `zones` sense), keyed on `venue.address.country` as ESPN spells it, covering those 36 strings and the five domestic countries (all five are in the list); a `VENUE_TZ_OVERRIDES` map keyed by ESPN venue id, empty this season, with a comment naming what would populate it (a Canary or Madeira side, a neutral venue), checked before the country map; both applied in `normalizeEvent` for every competition, so the competition `tz` field stops meaning "a fixture's clock" and its comment says what it now means.
