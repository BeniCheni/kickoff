**Reproduced.** Round 2's command against the real `normalizeEvent` → `fixtureTimes` returns `Europe/Zurich · 9:00 PM · 8:00 PM · 3:00 PM` for Anfield event `401915446` (stored local, true local, Brooklyn). **What the provider gives.** `competitions[0].venue` carries `id`, `fullName`, `address.city`, `address.country` and no zone (option C is closed: no time-zone field exists on the event, the competition or the venue). Across the three competitions' whole league phases (396 events), the 108 clubs use **36 country strings** as ESPN spells them: England, Spain, Germany, France, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal, Belgium, Greece, Türkiye, Czechia, Norway, Austria, Denmark, Poland, Cyprus, Romania, Bulgaria, Scotland, Croatia, Switzerland, Slovakia, Armenia, Slovenia, Hungary, Albania, Finland, Sweden, Latvia, Kazakhstan, Georgia, Andorra, Lithuania, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Gibraltar. The city is not a key: Signal Iduna Park is filed under "Aue".

**Multi-zone countries in this season's 108.** None in practice: every Spanish and Portuguese club is on the mainland (Atlético, Barcelona, Celta, Getafe, Betis, Real Madrid, Real Sociedad, Villarreal; Benfica, Braga, Porto, Sporting, Torreense), and Kairat Almaty sits in a country that has used one zone (`Asia/Almaty`) since 2024. The per-venue override list is therefore **empty this season and structurally required**: a Canary or Madeira side, or a neutral venue, breaks a country-only map. The domestic path has the same shape today (`venueTz = COMPETITIONS[key].tz`, `espn.ts` line 166) and is correct only because no Big-5 club plays outside its country's primary zone; La Liga 2026-27 has no Canary club (checked against `standings.json`).

**Option B** (a per-club home-zone table keyed by ESPN team id) needs 108 rows this season and grows with every qualifier, and it is wrong the day a club plays a home tie at a neutral venue (a sanction, a stadium closure) because the zone follows the club, not the ground. Rejected on those two counts.

**Recommended: option A.** A hand-authored country → IANA reference table beside `LEAGUE_TABLES` (reference data in the `tv` and `zones` sense), keyed on `venue.address.country` exactly as ESPN spells it, with a `VENUE_TZ_OVERRIDES` map keyed by ESPN venue id (empty, with a comment naming what would populate it), applied in `normalizeEvent` for every competition including domestic. The competition `tz` field then means "the competition's home zone for reference data", never a fixture's clock.

**The honest fallback, and why it needs code.** Today an absent zone does not crash: `fixtureTimes(iso, undefined)` lets `Intl` fall back to the viewer's system zone. Rendered in the pane at 390 in Ledger with one served fixture's `venueTz` set to `undefined` in memory (Liverpool v Fulham, kickoff 14:00Z; restored after), the expanded row printed **"🏴 10:00 AM local"** beside "🗽 10:00 AM EDT" — the viewer's own zone dressed as the stadium clock; true local is 3:00 PM. An empty string or a bogus zone throws `RangeError` inside render (caught by the view boundary). So the recommended state is explicit: `venueTz` becomes optional in the schema (`z.string().min(1).optional()`), `fixtureTimes` takes `venueTz: string | undefined` and returns `local: null` for it, the two renderers of the local clock handle null (`FixtureRow.tsx` line 113's local pill renders "local time not known" in the amber provenance voice; `TablePage.tsx` line 358's next lane drops the local half), the two Brooklyn-only consumers are unaffected by construction (`NextUpStrip`, `TonightSlate`), and the sync report gains a `zones-unknown=N` count. The diff engine does not compare `venueTz` today (`diff.ts` has `VENUE_CHANGED` on the venue name only), so a zone that changes between syncs is invisible; a `VENUE_TZ_CHANGED` kind, non-urgent, is part of the same change.

**Closure evidence for the build.** (1) The six MD1 counterexamples render 19:45 / 20:00 through the real functions with Brooklyn unchanged: done here with the probe map, to be repeated by the build's tests. (2) `tests/venueTz.test.ts`: every fixture in the committed snapshot has a `venueTz` consistent with its venue's country under the reference table (fails on the first unmapped country string ESPN introduces), and every domestic fixture's zone is unchanged by the change (a regression guard for hard rule 6's neighbour). ## Q2 — Round identity (finding 11, row 36, HONESTY rule 3). Blocks design approval and implementation.

**Reproduced.** The canvas's resolver returns `[1, null, 2]` for the same event on 9 Sep, 30 Sep and 13 Oct. The eight windows are disjoint and ordered. Clustering the 144 unique UCL events: Sep 8/9/10 (6/6/6), Oct 13/14, Oct 20/21, Nov 3/4, Nov 24/25, Dec 8/9, Jan 19/20 (9/9 each), Jan 27 (18); zero outside; largest inclusive 28-day slice **54** (Oct 13 → Nov 9). UEL: 144 events, MD1 on Sep 16/17 (9/9), then 18 on each of Oct 15, Oct 22, Nov 5, Nov 26, Dec 10, Jan 21, Jan 28. UECL: 108 events, six matchdays of 18 on Oct 15, Oct 22, Nov 5, Nov 26, Dec 10, Dec 17. No per-event round exists anywhere: `week` absent on all 144, `competitions[0].notes` empty on all 144, and the one text field that looked like a marker, `competitions[0].altGameNote`, reads "UEFA Champions League, League Phase" on every event. Phase is `events[].season.slug = league-phase`.

**Progress versus identity.** `max(played)` of 8 is progress and F2 supplies it. Identity is which matchday a fixture belongs to, and nothing synced carries it.

**What UEFA's regulations say (primary source, Q8).** Article 23.01: league-phase matches are played on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, except matchday 1, which spans Tuesday to Thursday. Article 27.01: a match that cannot start or be completed is, as a rule, played the next day. Article 27.02: if not, the UEFA administration fixes a new date. Consequence for the design: the common reschedule lands one day later. A Tuesday tie moved to Wednesday stays inside its two-day window and derives correctly; a Wednesday tie moved to Thursday, or a matchday-1 Thursday tie moved to Friday, falls outside every window and the canvas's absence state fires, which is honest. The silent case, a tie moved into another matchday's window, needs 27.02's new date to land on a later matchday's Tuesday or Wednesday. Possible, rarer than the design's 1c artboard implies, and not zero.

**Option A traced.** `scripts/sync.ts` (lines 112–117) carries hand-authored `note` forward by id before the diff; the same loop can carry `round`, deriving it from the windows only when absent, so the first sighting fixes the baseline. `src/lib/schema.ts` line 41 already has `round?: string`; `scripts/providers/espn.ts` lines 183–186 must change from "deliberately never set" to "set once from the published windows, never from the provider". `scripts/diff.ts` already emits `DATE_MOVED` when the Brooklyn date changes (lines 104–109); the render rule is `round !== resolve(kickoffUtc)` → "MATCHDAY n · RESCHEDULED", which needs no new diff kind. Three states result: COMPUTED (1c draws it), absent for a first sighting outside every window (1c draws it as "MATCHDAY — · DATE OUTSIDE UEFA'S PUBLISHED WINDOWS"), and RESCHEDULED (1c lacks it). The baseline needs the same season guard as the zones (`ZONES_SEASON`).

**The residual, quantified.** The league phase was drawn on 27 Aug 2026 (ESPN's calendar opens the phase at 2026-08-27T07:00Z; Wikipedia gives the Monaco draw date) and MD1 was played 8–10 Sep; activation cannot precede Beni's rulings, so the first sync that writes UCL rows is at least thirteen days after the draw and after one matchday. An event moved between windows in that interval is baselined wrong with a COMPUTED label. Today's 144 all sit inside a window and none carries a status other than scheduled, half-time or full-time, so no move is detectable from the feed, which is exactly the point: the residual cannot be measured from ESPN, only bounded by the calendar. Option B (no matchday) has no residual and no feature. Option C (a hand list of exceptions) is the file nobody can contradict.

**Tests to specify.** (1) In-window transfer: a fixture baselined MD1 whose kickoff moves to 13 Oct renders RESCHEDULED, not MD2. (2) Out-of-window first sighting renders the absence state. (3) First-seen-moved: a fixture first synced inside the MD2 window is baselined MD2 and the test names that as the accepted residual, so the assertion documents the cost rather than hides it. **Proposed rule 3 sentence:** "Matchday numbers are not invented either. Where the provider exposes none, the app derives the number once, from the governing body's published windows, on the day it first sees the fixture, labels it computed, and keeps it; a fixture whose date later leaves that window is shown as rescheduled from it, and a fixture first seen outside every window is shown without a number. A fixture already moved between windows before the app first saw it is numbered by the window it was found in, and that is the one case this rule cannot detect." **ESTIMATE: S** for option A. One-word question for Beni: **A, B or C.**

## Retro — what a technical-resolution pass needed that the earlier methods did not

- **A "what does the code do with nothing" probe.** Round 1 and Round 2 asked what a wrong zone renders; neither asked what an absent one does, and the answer (the viewer's own zone, silently) reframed the fallback from a nicety into the honesty rule. A resolution pass should run every new optional field through its renderers with `undefined`, `''` and garbage before proposing the schema.
- **Read the regulator before designing the exception.** Round 2 could not source UEFA's policy; two fetches of `documents.uefa.com` settled that reschedules land "the next day, as a rule", which decides which of the two failure cases is common. The method should say: for any hand-authored window or rule, fetch the governing body's article first and let it choose the cases the tests cover.
- **A structural change is proved layout-neutral by a box diff, not by inspection.** Grouping the live DOM and comparing every row and divider box before and after was cheap and is the only thing that makes "domestic tables unchanged" a measurement rather than a promise. The build's review should repeat it.
- **Inventory the provider's whole population before proposing a map.** Pulling all 396 events across three competitions found the one club with no venue at all and confirmed the override list is empty this season; a map proposed from the eighteen MD1 events would have missed both.
- **An acceptance criterion can have a blast radius the design never saw.** The standings guard is correct for the Table and can take the whole snapshot down in February; a resolution pass should trace each new guard to the boundary it lives inside.
