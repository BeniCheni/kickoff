## 1. Executive recommendation

The design ambition survives Round 2 intact. All four rulings on the canvas stand as drawn: the derived matchday with an amber provenance line, `--comp-ucl` unchanged, the 36-row table with a scoped sticky region header, and Moments as a linked still with the empty state first. What did not survive is the premise the canvas led with: ESPN serves fixtures and 36-entry League Phase standings for all three UEFA competitions in the shape the existing normaliser reads, and the "Beni's curl check" attribution was never a check. The design is therefore approvable against a known provider, not a hypothetical one, once the canvas is corrected.

Three things stand between the canvas and approval, and none is cosmetic. First, finding 18: the two-entry activation path (`espnCode` plus `LEAGUE_TABLES.ucl`) gives six of eighteen MD1 fixtures a stadium clock one hour wrong, because the normaliser assigns the competition's Zurich zone to every venue; this is the app's founding promise and it needs a venue-zone policy before a build is authorised. Second, finding 11: a tie moved into another matchday's window is misnumbered silently, and the amended rule 3 as written would let the app say "COMPUTED" over a wrong number; the policy for that case has to be chosen, not discovered. Third, the two load-bearing product rulings the canvas left open, where Moments live (c) and what the sticky divider does on desktop (g), plus the provider ruling (a), which the evidence has narrowed to one word.

Recommended path, in order: commission one bounded CTO technical-resolution pass now (docs and disposable probes only; the prompt is written and attached); on its evidence Beni makes the ten decisions in section 8, most of them one word; then the VIP correction and sign-off prompt goes to Claude Design from those rulings; then the implementation prompt is written from the signed-off canvas revision and Beni's scope decision; then a Codex build, a Claude cold review, and a release with its own verification. Do not start implementation before the venue-clock and matchday-exception policies are ruled.

Release placement, provisional: keep v0.4.0 as the sync theme Beni already ruled, and ship "Kickoff learns the European week" (the canvas's own title) as the next minor after it, provisionally v0.5.0, unified with Moments unless the CTO finds a concrete dependency that argues for staging. Ideas row 29 (auto-merged syncs never reach Pages) is a deploy-correctness prerequisite for any flagship release and belongs with v0.4.0's sync plumbing. The natural user-value date is UCL matchday 2, Tue 13 Oct 2026; no delivery date is promised until the implementing seat estimates.
